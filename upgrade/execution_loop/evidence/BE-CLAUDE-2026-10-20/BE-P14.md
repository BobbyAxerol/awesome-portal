1. `apps/control-api/test/elp2-manager-scoped-world.harness.ts` drives the real `ScopedManagerReader` + real `ExecutionReadScopes` + real access evaluation + real `KeysetCursorCodec` over plain TEST_ONLY worlds (`packages/contracts/tooling/execution-manager-scoped-world.mjs`). 2. `elp2-manager-scoped-fixtures.spec.ts` in capture mode (`CAPTURE_MANAGER_SCOPED=1`) writes `tooling/execution-manager-scoped-capture.json`; in verify mode (default) it re-reads **every scenario and every refusal** from the live reader and compares (cursors by what they bind: resource, workspace, direction, boundary; the query fingerprint is an opaque digest of scope internals). 9 tests pass.
3. `generate-execution-manager-scoped.mjs` (via `execution-manager-scoped-publish.mjs`) publishes the capture and **proves each case before writing**: the producer Zod validator, the published JSON Schema, the 1 MiB budget, and the real signed codec for every cursor (it binds the page edge; one fingerprint per chain). Every mutation negative must be refused by the real validator or generation fails; the codec-level refusals are re-proved with the real codec.
4. Re-capture command (when the reader legitimately changes): `cd apps/control-api && CAPTURE_MANAGER_SCOPED=1 vitest run test/elp2-manager-scoped-fixtures.spec.ts`, then `node packages/contracts/tooling/generate-execution-manager-scoped.mjs`.

### Refusals (each proves its code)
Schema-level (20, generator-proved): version mismatch, four extra-sensitive-field cases (facts, envelope, page, revision metadata), claims of ALL coverage / GLOBAL totals / COMPLETE, `DISCONNECTED` state, item environment `all`, money as number / exponent, closed enums, control characters, a cursor with a space, `has_more` without a cursor, `returned_count` above the items. **Schema looser than the producer in three recorded places** (`no-dispositions`, `has-more-without-cursor`, `returned-count-above-items`): the schema has no `minItems:1` on `profile_dispositions` and no cross-field rule. Not changed (tightening a published schema is a contract decision); the FE reader already enforces the page rules (SS-2).

Runtime (15, reader-proved; the 5 cursor cases also codec-proved): `CURSOR_CONTEXT_MISMATCH` 400 (changed query, wrong direction, other workspace, new source revision), `INVALID_CURSOR` 400 (tampered), `CURSOR_EXPIRED` 400 (901 s), `EXECUTION_READ_ACCESS_CHANGED` 409 (revoked mid-request), `EXECUTION_READ_AUTHORITY_UNAVAILABLE` 503 (named environment not recorded; **an item that would need 201 currency totals is refused whole, never truncated**), `EXECUTION_READ_NOT_AVAILABLE` 404 (not granted, all environments denied, unknown binding) and 400 (a hidden field is not a search oracle), `EXECUTION_READ_POLICY_PENDING` 403, `EXECUTION_READ_FIELD_POLICY_PENDING` 503.

### Findings worth the lead's attention
- **FE reader defect the BINDINGS fixture will expose.** `scopedRead.ts:265` keys SS-4 (a row once) on `[environment, alpha_id, alpha_version_id, portfolio_id]`. A BINDINGS item has none of those (its id is `identity.binding_id`), so B1 and B2 of one environment collide and the read is refused. The previous fixture (BINDINGS empty) could not show it.
- Per-currency totals are **per item**; the same shared account is deliberately stated on one item only (the binding). A client that sums `financial_totals` across items double counts nothing here, but must still never present the sum as a fund NAV (`totals_scope: VISIBLE_ROWS_ONLY`).
- `is_full_resource` is `true` for the researcher's own alphas (the producer's rule); it does not mean the account balance is complete.

### What this round implements, and why that is "nothing in the producer"
**Implemented: nothing in the producer or the published contract.** The only items the Portal holds with a clear authority and no new policy decision are R-5a (unit = UTC epoch ms) and R-5d (basis) as *documentation + const members*, and R-7 allocation (already published). Everything else needs a `BUSINESS_READ_FIELDS` entry plus a researcher-whitelist line plus a closed-schema member, which changes what every reader may see (whole-domain readers get new fields the moment they are in `BUSINESS_READ_FIELDS`, `read-scope.ts:816`), and FE readers are strict. That is a policy decision, not a fixture. R-5a/R-5d would regenerate `execution-scoped-records.openapi.json`, the d.ts and the EDS-12/N29 pins, which this task must not commit; they are the first additive change to ask Codex to schedule. **Must stay typed absence** (the source has no column or no stated meaning): alpha name/version/owner/stage; sandbox/live per-deployment net PnL/exposure; order-level fee; bracket leg type/entry link; `finding_type` classification.

## 0. Rules used in the matrix

- Profile. The fixture BFF default today is `FIXTURE_REVIEW_PROFILE=sparse-realistic` (`apps/portal/fixture-bff/app.ts loadConfig`). In that profile `answerReviewManager` (`apps/portal/fixture-bff/review-manager.ts`) is skipped and the `simulated` overlays (`apps/portal/frontend/e2e/bffSimulated.ts`, `bffSimulatedStage.ts`) are off. Positive Manager data, the WP-A/B/C/E/F1/F2/F3/K fixture keys and the lit stage bodies exist only in `populated` or `layout-stress`. The WP-P positive profile is not in this tree (`ReviewProfile` in `review-catalog.ts` is `sparse-realistic | populated | layout-stress`). So COVERED below means: served by the producer named, in the profile named, and drawn by the panel named.
- Dictionary column. The field dictionary (`packages/contracts/fixtures/execution-publication-source/field-dictionary.json`, 31 fields) has no family-to-field mapping: `registry.json` families carry id, owner, grain, gates and consumer, but no field list, and `field_sources[]` names `source_ref` and mapping refs per field, not a family. Every dictionary entry in the matrix is therefore INFERRED from `source_ref`/name/unit, and "none in dictionary" is literal: the 31 fields are Manager metric and identity fields only (new_risk_capacity, amount_locked, recall_pending, alpha_id, alpha_version_id, owner_user_id, portfolio_id, financial_stage, lifecycle, allocation_weight, management_budget, margin_risk_requirement, economic_backing, exposure_cap, pnl, unrealized_pnl, account_balance, equity30d_end, return30d_pct, health, source_status, raw_q, contribution, alpha_score, portfolio_score, regime_distance, initial_funds, added_funds, returned_funds, nav, as_of_ms). Most families (orders, fills, postings, journal, audit, locks, recovery, reports) have no dictionary field at all.
- Statuses. COVERED = positive data served and drawn by the named path. PARTIAL = some of the family's facts are served and drawn; the cell says what is missing (and whether the missing part is a contract place, a fixture, or a panel). CONTRACT_GAP = no Portal DTO has a place; the cell names the DTO that should carry it. FIXTURE_GAP = DTO exists, no positive producer. UI_GAP = data served, not drawn. Sub-gaps of the last two kinds are named inside PARTIAL cells.
- Receipt. A "browser receipt" is a Playwright run on the default positive scenario at 1280 and 660, with: the named route/panel populated with positive values, zero overflow, no typed absence on a panel whose contract place exists, no request outside the fixture BFF, and each linked id clicked through to its target. None exists for any row; the cell says which receipt is needed.

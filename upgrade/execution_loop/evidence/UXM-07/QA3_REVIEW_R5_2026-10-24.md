**R5-01 BLOCKER, canary (and every page that mentions it), all widths, FIXTURE (WP P).** The canary deployment has two identities. The room is `dep_canary_01` (`canary-1440-t01.png` breadcrumb x 444-525 y 195) with order ids `ord_dep_b3_1`, `ord_dep_b3_2` (`canary-positions-1440-t02.png` y 446-478). Clicking `/deployments/live/dep_b3` opens a generic "Live operations" page "dep_b3 · BINANCE · Alpha Not available", breadcrumb pf_crypto / acct_live_grid, everything "Not available". The owner cannot get from Alpha or Account to the canary room, and the id he sees on the room is not the id the rest of the world uses. Design: one deployment, one id/name, reachable from its alpha and account. Fix: single deployment id in the world (dep_b3 or dep_canary_01), alpha and account link to the room.

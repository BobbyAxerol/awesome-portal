Annotations
  1. Identity and back path. Breadcrumb reads by display name, IDs stay in mono for routing. "Back to Fleet results" keeps the exact query so the list returns to the same place. The header holds only identity: owner, binding, evidence, stage presence. The researcher owner is the author; deployers appear per deployment row only.
  2. Stage presence is a state, not a total: Live and Paper are active now, Canary and Sandbox ended and are shown muted so the history is still readable.
  3. Domain, window, partition. Real-funded and simulated paths are separate choices; there is no combined view. The partition chip says money is USDT real-funded only.
  4. Requested vs effective window. The user asked for 30D but only 10 days of actual money exist, so the card says "requested 30D, effective 10D" instead of quietly stretching it.
  5. QBTS card. Every score is policy pending in real data today, so "Pending" is the first state, not an error. No band or number. "Explain" opens the drawer, where the future layout is a clearly labelled variant.
  6. Regime Fit card is a sibling of the score, not a child. Insufficient data and unbound are real states with their own chips; neither blocks the economics.
  7. Tabs follow the guide: Performance, Deployments, Research Evidence, Regime Fit, Orders & Fills, Activity & Decisions. Tab is kept in the URL (?tab=). Deployments and Orders & Fills link to the existing detail screens, which are unchanged.
  8. Three path facets, never merged. Actual economic path, forward simulated (Paper, Sandbox) and Research OOS are switchable facets with their own captions. No simulated line is ever drawn on or converted into actual equity. The equity chart needs a manager time-series endpoint (FE-013).
  9. Cross-stage timeline. Segment widths are proportional to days. Hatching means simulated, solid means actual. Per-segment cards carry version/config, source, costs and return (Modified-Dietz). Money is not added across segments, because Paper and Sandbox sit in other partitions.
  10. Forward observation facet has a different frame (dashed, hatched title) so it cannot be mistaken for a track record. Observed 70d and actual 10d sit side by side. The mixed return stays "Not available" until a continuity manifest is verified, and no mixed score reaches the headline.
  11. Regime Fit panel. Six support states as plain pills with text. The panel opens on Insufficient data (today), then Unbound (digest mismatch, never "latest run"), then a Supported variant marked Future. TradeState is a separate engine fact.
  12. Score drawer. Built for "policy pending" first: raw QBTS, contribution (w_i × q_i, no band), band, coverage, components, evidence and policy, all with exact reasons. The Future layout toggle shows where band, provisional badge, P/R/B and contribution will go. Policy version is "Not published" today.
  13. Backend limits honoured. Returns are Modified-Dietz (FE-006). Score sort is hidden while policy is pending (FE-011). No text search or cross-environment totals are assumed.
  14. Alternative considered: one long page with the drawer as a modal and all six tabs flattened into sections. Rejected: it buries the cross-stage timeline under Regime Fit and Evidence, and a modal hides the metrics the explanation is about.

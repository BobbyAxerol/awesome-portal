## Addendum — one chart (Bobby's direction, same day)

Bobby asked what the separate account-observation chart was for: the chart should draw orders, trades, fills, conditional orders, order types and the limit / stop levels. Decision taken (Bobby chose "cây OCO"; the chart answer was this direction): **one chart** on native routes.

- `ExecuteDetail.tsx`: the separate "OOS observation replay" panel is no longer rendered on a native route (other fixed routes keep it). Its equity and native position are now two lower panes of the same chart; its readout (OHLC, position, equity) is in the chart readout.
- `WindowFillCandles.tsx` (the accepted lightweight-charts host): order tracks drawn as horizontal segments from the instant the source made an order working (`activate`, else `place`) to its terminal lifecycle event, one two-point series per order (a line never joins two orders); a limit entry solid, a stop and a reduce-only limit dashed, a withdrawn order (cancelled / expired / rejected) grey; fills ▲ buy / ▼ sell at their own price in ink so they read on green and red candles; `cancel` marks; the readout lists the orders working at the hovered bar with type, level (trigger named as a trigger), parent, OCO group and how/when it ended; ← → Shift Home End step the readout.
- `executionTracks.ts` (pure, tested on the real journal): tracks from `result.orders` joined to the command report and lifecycle events **by explicit `order_id` only**; states from the source's terminal event (`fill`, `cancel`, `expire`, `reject`); no role (entry / stop-loss / take-profit) is stated that the source did not name — a track says its order type and "reduce-only".
- `OcoTree.tsx` (HiFi §2.9): groups by explicit `oco_group_id`, parent from `parent_order_id`, legs struck through when cancelled / expired with the instant, the filled leg with its fill.
- `WindowReplayCard.tsx`: ORDER / COMMAND / LIFECYCLE / FILL are read for the chart overlay, every page up to 5 × 200 rows per relation (a longer relation says so; never cut silently), independent of the journal table's own page.

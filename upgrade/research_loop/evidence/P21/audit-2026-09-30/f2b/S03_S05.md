## Decisions
1. `window` in the query, fold in the hash (see above), default view when absent is S08's `oos`.
2. The HOLDOUT link is offered even when the holdout is withheld / not applicable (WFO): S08 answers with the typed absence. No bar is read, the tooltip says so.
3. The bands are links with `tabIndex=-1`; the legend labels are the keyboard path (no duplicate tab stops).
4. `next: Evidence Flow →` is shown whenever the full screen renders (running too): Evidence Flow already handles a live run; hiding it left the page a dead end until seal. Not shown on not-found / queued-without-source.
5. Fold buttons in S05 (gantt, θ* lanes) stay cursor pickers; the replay link lives in the table row because an anchor cannot sit inside a button.

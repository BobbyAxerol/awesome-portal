## Decisions
- Rules footer removed as a whole (VD-TEXT-01 text-trim, §5.1), its words kept in the h2 tooltip: it is the one HiFi footer the user's "less hard text" removes first.
- Confirm sentences kept short, not deleted: they gate irreversible writes.
- Pick approval link only from the mirror's served href (never a FE-built governance URL): FE must not invent routes/permissions. Without a served href the chip is plain.
- Pick trial link carries no fold: the pick has no fold field, so none is invented.
- Compose -> publish -> back: after publish the way out is header `run report` / `lineage` and the mirror's Approval Inbox link; pack route returns via the crumb or "open the run's composition".

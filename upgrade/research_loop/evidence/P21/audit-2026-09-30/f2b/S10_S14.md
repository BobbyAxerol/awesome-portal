## Decisions

- Compare for a root attempt goes to the alpha-scoped Screen 13 (cannot preselect one run; `compareHref` needs a pair).
- No auto-navigation after register: activation is a separate permissioned act and the registration receipt would be lost; instead the next step is the primary button.
- Header "next: New Run" carries only the alpha before registration (never a guessed version).
- `LineageLink` got optional `title`, `primary`, `extra` props (additive). `newRunHref` in AlphaImport now delegates to new `newRunPrefillHref` in `lineageClient.ts` (additive: `newRunPrefillHref`, `alphaImportHref`).
- Did not touch `codeAnatomy.ts` (copy stays there, now shown as tooltip).
- Visible hard-coded text on S10 live was already small (55 words); the bigger text win was S14.

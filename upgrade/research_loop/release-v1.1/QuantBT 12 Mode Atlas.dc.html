A run's certificate = min(engine cert of its contract, methodology cap of its mode × schedule, artifact completeness). The two chips on the Release Candidate screen carry the axes separately.
rule one The claim comes from mode × schedule, never from the mode alone. mode_1_decay + per_fold_causal is strict outer OOS; the same mode with per_fold_decay is selection-adjusted. Click the schedule chips below and watch the claim change.
rule two Two selector layers, never mixed: optimization_mode (this page) belongs to the walk-forward endpoint; CandidateSelector.mode (best · feasible_best · pareto_first · robust_plateau) belongs to the optimization package and appears only inside Stage 2's decision card.

when to reach for which mode
mode_1_decayThe deployment path. Per-fold nested search; the schedule decides whether the OOS evidence is strict or selection-adjusted. Everything before Approval Gate should end here.
mode_2_sbbStress a candidate against synthetic regimes before spending OOS on it. Report-only by construction — its bootstrap never touches a held-out bar.
mode_3_flat_minimaAsk whether a good score sits on a plateau or a spike. The right follow-up whenever best ≡ selected in mode 1.
mode_4_is_only_robustTemporal robustness without spending OOS: does θ* survive every IS subperiod, or only the average of them?
mode_5_full_robustCalibration on everything, claims about nothing. Use it to shape a search space, then open a real mode_1 run.
rules of this screen Claim strings are the engine's, rendered verbatim from the mode × schedule matrix — the UI never composes its own. Invalid schedules render struck-through with the reason, never hidden. A real run locks mode and schedule at submit; this atlas exists so nobody learns that the hard way.

rules of this screen Three modes, three rules. Attempts of one alpha: overlay allowed, but claims rank before numbers and only the ACTIVE node may promote. One alpha across markets: keys differ ⇒ small multiples, no Δ, no overlay. Two alphas on one key: overlay + deltas are legal, correlation is engine-published, and the weaker claim of the pair gates any joint promotion. ComparisonContractKey is an engine artifact — the UI checks equality, it never constructs one.
